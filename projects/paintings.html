The method

Half of it is out of my hands

Alcohol ink does most of the composing on its own. On a surface that will not absorb it, the colour spreads, pools and pushes against whatever is already down, drying into cells and hard tidelines that no brush could draw. My job is to decide when to intervene and when to leave it alone.

What comes afterwards is deliberate: metallic ink drawn in a continuous line, opaque white lifted over the top, and the occasional figure — a jellyfish, a rose, a poppy — pulled out of what the ink had already suggested. The finished pieces are sealed to a high gloss, so the colour stays as wet-looking as it was on the day it was poured.

Framed works

Studies

Smaller sheets, worked quickly. Most of what ends up in the framed pieces is decided here first — which colours will hold each other off, how far the ink can be pushed before it turns to mud, where a drawn line will sit once the ground has dried.

Originals & commissions

If something here should be on your wall.

Originals come framed, and I take commissions — if you have a colour, a size or a room in mind, tell me and we can work it out. New pieces and work in progress go up on Instagram first.
